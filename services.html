Windshield Chip & Crack Repair

A rock chip is a small break in the outer layer of the windshield. Our technician cleans it out, pulls the air from the break with a vacuum, and fills it with a clear resin that cures under UV light.

Chip repair is for drivers who catch the damage early, while it is still smaller than a quarter. Left alone, heat and road bumps spread a chip into a crack that can only be fixed with a new windshield.

Windshield Replacement

Windshield replacement means cutting out the cracked glass, cleaning the frame down to a fresh bed of old urethane, priming, and setting new glass in new adhesive. Moldings, the mirror mount and rain sensor gel pads move over to the new glass.

You need replacement when a crack is long, sits in the driver's view, reaches the edge, or the glass is badly pitted. A cracked windshield is weaker in a rollover and can let a passenger airbag push the glass out.

ADAS Camera Calibration

ADAS stands for advanced driver assistance systems: lane-keep assist, automatic emergency braking, and adaptive cruise. Many of them rely on a camera mounted behind the windshield, and that camera has to be re-aimed when the glass changes.

Skipping calibration can leave a car that drifts in its lane, brakes for shadows, or misses a real hazard. Any car with a camera bracket behind the mirror needs it after a new windshield.

Call us today for ADAS calibration after windshield replacement. Read more about adas camera calibration.

Side & Back Glass Replacement

Side windows and back glass are usually tempered glass, which breaks into small cubes instead of cracking. We remove the door panel, vacuum out every piece, fit new glass in the regulator, and test the window up and down.

This service is for break-ins, storm damage, and windows that shattered after a door slam. An open window lets in rain and salt air, so it is worth covering fast.
