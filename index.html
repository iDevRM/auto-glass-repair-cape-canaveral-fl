The basics

What Is Auto Glass Repair in Cape Canaveral?

Auto glass repair covers anything that fixes or replaces the glass in your car, truck or SUV. That includes filling a rock chip with resin, swapping out a cracked windshield, replacing a shattered door window, and re-aiming the camera that sits behind the windshield.

Anyone who drives the A1A strip, the 528 causeway, or the bridges to Merritt Island picks up rock chips sooner or later. Salt air and sand also wear a fine haze into older glass. The goal is simple: clear, safe glass that holds the roof up in a rollover and lets your airbag deploy the way it should.

Warning signs

When Do You Need Windshield Repair or Replacement?

Most drivers call us after one of these moments. If any of them sound familiar, get the glass looked at before a small problem turns into a bigger one.

1

A rock hit on the causeway

Gravel from trucks on SR 528 and SR 520 leaves a star or bullseye chip. Fixed within a few days, it usually stays a repair.

2

A crack that keeps growing

A line that creeps a little further after every hot afternoon means the glass is under stress and will not stop on its own.

3

Storm or hurricane debris

Palm fronds, branches and flying debris during tropical weather can crack or shatter a windshield or side window.

4

A break-in or shattered door glass

Tempered side glass breaks into small cubes. The opening needs covering and the door needs a full clean-out.

5

Glare and haze you cannot clean off

Sand pitting and years of salt spray scatter headlight glare at night. When cleaning does not help, the glass is worn.

6

A warning light after glass work

A lane-keep or forward-collision warning after a windshield swap often means the camera was never recalibrated.

Auto Glass Questions From Cape Canaveral Drivers

How much does auto glass repair cost in Cape Canaveral?

The cost depends on your vehicle, the type of glass, and whether a camera or rain sensor sits behind it. A chip repair is a much smaller job than a full windshield. Call or send the quote form with your year, make and model and we will give you a free quote before any work starts.

Will my insurance pay for a new windshield in Florida?

Usually, yes. Florida Statute 627.7288 says the deductible on a comprehensive policy does not apply to windshield damage. If you carry comprehensive coverage, a covered windshield replacement typically costs you nothing out of pocket, and we can help you start the claim.

Can my windshield chip be repaired or does it need replacing?

Most chips smaller than a quarter and single cracks shorter than about six inches can be repaired. Damage right in the driver's line of sight, damage that reaches the edge of the glass, or several long cracks usually means a new windshield. We tell you which one you need before we start.

How long does a windshield replacement take?

The work itself takes about an hour. After that, the urethane adhesive needs time to reach a safe drive-away strength, usually under an hour on a warm, humid day. We give you the exact drive-away time for the adhesive we used.

Do you come to my home or work?

Yes. We are a mobile service, so the work happens in your driveway, condo lot, or office parking lot. You only need a level spot with room to open the doors, and a garage or carport if storms are moving in.

Does my car need a camera calibration after a new windshield?

If your car has lane-keep assist, automatic emergency braking, or adaptive cruise, there is likely a camera mounted behind the windshield. That camera has to be re-aimed after the glass is replaced so the safety features read the road correctly. We check your vehicle and tell you up front.

Why did my small chip turn into a long crack?

Heat is the most common cause. Glass that sits in the sun all day and then gets blasted with cold air conditioning flexes, and a chip gives that stress a place to spread. Potholes, slammed doors, and rough bridge expansion joints can do the same thing, so fix chips early.

Can you replace a windshield when it is raining?

The adhesive needs a dry surface, so we do not install glass in open rain. Summer storms here usually pass quickly, so we either work under a garage, carport, or covered parking deck, or move the appointment by a few hours.
