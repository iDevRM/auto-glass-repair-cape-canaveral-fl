ADAS calibration in Cape Canaveral re-aims the camera mounted behind your windshield after the glass is replaced. It is for drivers whose car has lane-keep assist, automatic emergency braking, or adaptive cruise control. When it is done, those systems see the road where it actually is, instead of a few degrees off.

What Does ADAS Camera Calibration Involve?

We start with a scan to read the system and any stored codes. A static calibration uses a printed target set at exact distances in front of the car on level ground. A dynamic calibration has the technician drive the car on well-marked roads while a scan tool watches the camera learn.

Many vehicles need one method, some need both. We follow the carmaker's procedure for your model and confirm the system is clear of faults before we hand the keys back.

When Do You Need ADAS Camera Calibration?

  • After any windshield replacement on a car with a camera behind the mirror.
  • When a lane-departure or collision warning light comes on after glass work.
  • After a front-end collision, alignment change, or suspension work.
  • When lane-keep pulls the car toward one side of the lane.

Why Does a New Windshield Throw Off the Camera?

The camera bracket is bonded to the glass itself. Even a perfect install can sit the new windshield a fraction of a degree differently from the old one.

At highway speed, a tiny tilt means the camera is judging distance and lane lines several feet off. Calibration teaches the camera its new angle.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my car has an ADAS camera?

Look behind your rearview mirror from outside the car. A black housing with a lens, often with a small window in the tint band, usually means a forward camera.

What happens if I skip calibration?

Lane-keep may steer you toward the line, and automatic braking may react late or brake for nothing. A warning light may or may not appear, so do not rely on the dash to tell you.

Does insurance pay for ADAS calibration?

When calibration is required after a covered windshield replacement, insurers usually treat it as part of the claim. We confirm it with them before the work.

Can you calibrate without replacing the windshield?

Yes. If a warning light came on after other repairs, or a previous installer skipped calibration, we can scan and calibrate on its own.
