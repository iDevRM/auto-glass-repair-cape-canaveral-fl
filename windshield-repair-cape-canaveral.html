Windshield chip repair in Cape Canaveral saves your original windshield by filling a rock chip with clear resin before it can spread. It is for any driver with a fresh bullseye, star or small crack, and it takes about half an hour at your home or workplace. When it is done, the chip is sealed, much harder to see, and no longer growing.

What Does Windshield Chip & Crack Repair Involve?

The technician cleans loose glass and dirt out of the break, then mounts a bridge tool over it. The tool pulls a vacuum to get air out of the chip and pushes resin into every leg of the break.

A UV lamp cures the resin hard, and the surface is scraped flush and polished. You can drive right away, and there is no adhesive cure time like there is with a new windshield.

When Do You Need Windshield Chip & Crack Repair?

  • You have a chip smaller than a quarter or a single crack shorter than about six inches.
  • The damage is fresh. Dirt and moisture in an old chip make the repair less clear.
  • The chip is not right in front of the driver's eyes or touching the edge of the glass.

Why Do Small Windshield Chips Spread?

A windshield is two layers of glass with a plastic layer between them. A rock knocks out a tiny piece of the outer layer, and the edges of that break are weak points.

Heat makes glass expand. A windshield that bakes in a beach parking lot and then gets hit with cold air conditioning flexes unevenly, and the chip is where it gives. Potholes and bridge joints add more stress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the chip disappear completely after repair?

No, but it gets much harder to see. Most repaired chips fade to a faint mark, and the main goal is that the break is sealed and will not spread.

Can you repair a crack, or only chips?

We can repair a single crack up to about six inches long if it is not at the edge of the glass. Longer cracks, or cracks that started at the edge, need a new windshield.

Should I put tape over my windshield chip?

Clear tape over a fresh chip keeps dirt and water out until we get there, which helps the resin bond. Do not use glue or a drugstore kit if you plan to have it professionally repaired.

Does chip repair work in Florida heat?

Yes. We shade the glass while we work because resin cures fastest under UV light and flows best when the glass is not scorching hot.
